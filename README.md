# Objectnimator 🎭

Did you realize you want to create, for example, an **Object Show**, but the animation programs you want to use are premium or their free versions are very limited? 

Well, here is **Objectnimator**! A free and open-source app to animate your object shows. **YOU** can help and provide ideas; if there is a problem, even **YOU** can fix it with coding.

Made with essential libraries for modern animation, such as [OpenFL](https://www.openfl.org) and [Theatre.js](https://www.theatrejs.com).

---

## ✨ Silly & Cool Features

Objectnimator is built for making those smooth (and not-so-smooth) 2D moves:

*   **🎨 Rainbow Tinting (ColorTransform):** Make your characters turn red when they're mad, blue when they're sad, or a ghostly green! Just like the old Flash days. 🌈
*   **📐 Matrix Math Magic:** Everything is calculated with fancy matrices for pixel-perfect moves. No more shaky assets! 
*   **🔗 Infinite Parenting (The "Glue" System):** Stick arms to bodies, hats to heads, or cake to a plate! Link anything to everything with our `parentId` system. *Boing!*
*   **🌀 Squish & Stretch (Skew):** Give them that rubbery look with Skew, Scale, and Rotation in real-time. *Pop!*
*   **🎭 The "Pro" Timeline:** Smooth keyframes for everyone! No more frame-by-frame suffering.
*   **📂 Asset Swapping:** Changing facial expressions is as easy as clicking a button. 
*   **✍️ Chatty Text:** Make dialogues and also Subtitles :D. 🗣️

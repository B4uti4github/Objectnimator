# Objectnimator 🎭 v1.2

Did you realize you want to create an **Object Show**, but most animation programs are either "pay-to-win" or feel like they're from the stone age? 

Well, here is **Objectnimator**! A free and open-source app specifically designed to animate your object shows with modern tech. **YOU** are the director here: provide ideas, report bugs, or even fix them yourself!

Made with the ultimate "Flash-Killer" stack: [OpenFL](https://www.openfl.org) for the engine and [Theatre.js](https://www.theatrejs.com) for the professional timeline.

---

## ✨ Silly & Cool Features (v1.2)

Objectnimator is built for making those smooth (and not-so-smooth) 2D moves:

*   **🎞️ Native Video Export (NEW!):** Turn your Canvas into actual video files (.webm). It's silent (perfect for Mime characters!), but incredibly smooth! 🎬
*   **💾 Save & Load System:** Your projects aren't ghosts anymore. Save your progress into JSON files and keep cooking later. 📁
*   **🎨 Rainbow Tinting (ColorTransform):** Make characters turn red when mad, blue when sad, or ghostly green! Professional RGBA selectors included. 🌈
*   **📐 Matrix Math Magic:** Everything is calculated with fancy matrices for pixel-perfect moves. It’s not just math; it’s **Objectnimator Math**! 📐🔥
*   **🔗 Infinite Parenting (The "Glue" System):** Stick arms to bodies or hats to heads with our `parentId` system. Parenting so good, your assets won't need a babysitter. 👶🔗
*   **🌀 Squish & Stretch (Skew):** Give them that rubbery "Object Show" look with Skew, Scale, and Rotation in real-time. *Pop!*
*   **🎭 The "Pro" Timeline:** Smooth keyframes for everyone! Stop frame-by-frame suffering and let Theatre.js handle the curves.
*   **📂 Asset Swapping:** Expressions, limbs, and assets can be swapped with a single click.
*   **✍️ Chatty Text:** Built-in system for Dialogues and Subtitles. 🗣️

## 🎞️ The Render Lab (Adding Sound)

Since **Objectnimator v1.2** focus is on pixel-perfect visual rendering, the output video is silent. 

To complete your Object Show, simply import your `.webm` render (or convert into a mp4 if needed) into any video editor (like **CapCut, Kdenlive, Premiere, or DaVinci Resolve**) to add your voice acting, music, and sound effects! 📺🔊
